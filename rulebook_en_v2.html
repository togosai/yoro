Chapter 1: Tournament Overview
1. Tournament Summary

東合祭 -溶炉- (TOGOSAI -yoro-) is a 3-player team competition targeting Arknights' "Integrated Strategies #5: Sarkaz's Furnaceside Fables".

Each team member completes one official run. Final team standings are determined strictly by the total sum of Tournament Points (TP) earned by all three members.

Important: In-game scores are NOT used for rank determination; only our custom TP scoring system applies. Teams must strategically assign Squads and 6★ Operators among members without overlap, making team strategy and role distribution just as vital as individual skill.

Chapter 2: Competitive Rules
6. Basic Rules

This tournament is conducted as a 3-player team match. Each member performs exactly one official run, and final team rank is determined by the total accumulated TP of all 3 members.

【Squad & 6★ Operator Restrictions】
Within the same team, duplicate use of the same Squad or the same 6★ Operator is strictly prohibited.
*Pre-submission of chosen Squads and 6★ Operators is NOT required. As long as there is no overlap within the team at the moment the run begins, it is valid.
【Prospective Investment Restriction】
The total amount of Originium Ingot that can be withdrawn from the Investment System by the entire team is restricted to a maximum of 200 Originium Ingots per team.
*Teams must manage and ensure that the combined withdrawal amount across all 3 runners does not exceed 200 Originium Ingots.
7. Tournament Points (TP)

TP is awarded for designated Emergency Operations, Floor 3 Empowered Bosses, and Endings. Normal Operations sharing the same stage name do NOT award TP.

  • 【Stage Clear Prerequisite】
    Clearing the target stage is an absolute prerequisite for earning any TP. If a stage is not cleared (retreated or failed), no TP—including base clear TP or secondary bonus TP (e.g. Floor 5 spawn bonus, Fractured bonus)—will be awarded. When multiple scoring criteria are met upon clearing a stage, all corresponding TP values are cumulatively added.
  • 【Leakless Rule Exception (Enemy Unit 'Finale Cadence')】
    In Emergency Operations, if the ONLY enemy unit that enters the Objective Zone is "Finale Cadence" (meaning zero other enemies leaked), the run is exceptionally treated as Leakless. (*In "Fate's Finale", this exception does NOT apply, and leaking "Finale Cadence" counts as a normal leak.)
  • 【Duplicate Emergency Operation Clearance】
    If the same Emergency Operation is cleared multiple times in a single run, TP does NOT stack. Only the single highest TP score achieved on that stage will be counted.
  • Passing through "Holy City" results in a final TP of 0 points for that runner.
  • Using "Ingots Squad" or "Blueprint Squad" incurs a -40 TP penalty on the runner's final score.

9. Standings & Tie-breakers

Final team standings are determined by total team TP. Ties resolved via lowest TP ➔ highest TP.

10. Time Limit

Each runner has a 3-hour limit. Battles started before 3h mark can be finished.

11. Run Starting Position
Regardless of the selected Squad, the official run starts when the runner confirms the Floor 1 map.
*Node refreshing when confirming the Floor 1 map (before starting the run) is strictly prohibited.
12. Retry & Reroll Procedure

Rules regarding Retries and Rerolls are defined as follows:

【Standard Retry Rule】
Runners may retry 1 time before entering a combat node on Floor 3 to reroll Operation Rewards.
*Only during this 1st Retry, the 3-hour timer is reset once.
【Retry Rule for Missing "Fated Encounter"】
If the event "Fated Encounter" does not spawn at the first "Uncanny Encounter" node on Floor 1, runners may reroll Operation Rewards.
  • This specific reroll has unlimited attempts.
  • The 3-hour timer will NOT be reset.
  • If "Fated Encounter" still does not spawn after using the Standard Retry, runners may continue rerolling for Operation Rewards without timer resets.

Chapter 3: Operation & Management
14. Team Communication

Team members are strongly encouraged to communicate freely via Voice Chat (Discord VC) during runs. Single-player runs are permitted. Staff members in VC must be muted by runners during competition.

15. Streaming & Discord Rules

Screen sharing on the official tournament Discord is mandatory. Personal streaming is permitted, provided runners do NOT read chat/comments or receive stream advice during the match.

16. Main Event Flow

A maximum of 2 runners will compete sequentially per day (no parallel runs). All runners must join the Discord server prior to gathering time. Late arrivals may wait up to 30 minutes before rescheduling to the reserve day.

17. Pre-recording Rules

Runners unable to attend live matches may submit pre-recorded runs upon prior approval (Window: Aug 22 – Sep 20, 2026).

  • Must apply in advance, join designated Discord VC, share screen, and obey live tournament rules.
  • Only video stitching (joining multiple raw files) is allowed. Cut editing or content alteration is forbidden.
  • Switching to pre-recording after the Main Event begins is not permitted.
18. Penalties & Disqualification

Rule violations will result in a 0 TP score or full team disqualification depending on severity.

Main Subjects: Acquiring banned Collectibles, outside strategy coaching, cheating, pilot play, or unsportsmanlike behavior.
19. Miscellaneous

Unspecified situations or emergency rulings are subject to final decision by the Executive Committee. Team withdrawals must be reported immediately.

Chapter 4 FAQ
【Application & Team Setup】
QCan tournament staff help me find teammates?
No, staff will not introduce or match players. Please recruit teammates via SNS (X/Twitter) using hashtag "#東合祭".
QDo we need to pre-submit our Squads and 6★ Operators?
No pre-submission is required. As long as there is no overlap among team members when the official run starts, it is valid.
【Competitive Rules】
QIs node refreshing allowed before starting the run?
No. Node refreshing when confirming the Floor 1 map (before starting the run) is prohibited.
QIf a 6★ Operator is recruited via temporary recruitment, can another team member recruit that same Operator?
No. Under no circumstances can 6★ Operators overlap within the team. This rule also applies when recruited via Doctor Silver Seal.
QWhat if "Fated Encounter" does not spawn at 1st Uncanny Encounter?
Runners may reroll Operation Rewards infinitely. However, timer will not reset.
QUntil when can a Retry be declared?
A Retry is permitted exactly once before entering the first battle node on Floor 3. Retries are not allowed after that point.
QDoes the 3-hour timer continue ticking during rerolling?
Yes. Only the first retry declaration resets the 3-hour timer. Subsequent reroll attempts consume time continuously.

【TP & Standings】
QIf an enemy leaks while using "Ho'olheyak" equipped with Stage α Module, is it considered "Leakless"?
Yes. In that case, because the effect of Operator Ho'olheyak's Stage α Module prevents Life Points from decreasing, it is treated as "Leakless".
QDo Normal Operations sharing stage names award TP?
No. Only designated Emergency Operations, Floor 3 Empowered Bosses, and Endings listed in the scoring table award TP.
QCan I receive floor spawn bonuses or status bonuses if I fail to clear the stage?
No. Clearing the target stage is an absolute prerequisite for receiving any TP. If a stage is failed or retreated, no TP is awarded.
QHow is TP calculated when fulfilling multiple conditions in one stage?
All fulfilled criteria are cumulatively added upon clearing. (e.g. Clearing "Heavenly Paradise" on Floor 5 grants Clear (+40) + Floor 5 Spawn (+60) = 100 TP.)
QIf an enemy leaks in an Emergency Operation, do I lose the "Leakless" bonus?
In Emergency Operations, if "Finale Cadence" is the SOLE enemy that leaked, the stage exceptionally qualifies for the "Leakless" bonus.
QIs leaking "Finale Cadence" in "Fate's Finale" also treated as Leakless?
No. The "Finale Cadence" sole-leaker exception applies ONLY to Emergency Operations. In "Fate's Finale", leaking "Finale Cadence" will break Leakless status as normal.
QIf I clear the same Emergency Operation multiple times in one run, do TP stack?
No. Duplicate clearances of the same Emergency Operation do NOT stack. Only the single highest TP score achieved on that stage is counted.
